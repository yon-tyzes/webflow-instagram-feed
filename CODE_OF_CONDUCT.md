# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Everyone who takes part in this project, whether in issues, pull requests, or discussions, is expected to:

- Treat others with respect, including people who disagree with you.
- Give and accept feedback about the work, not the person.
- Assume good intent and ask before concluding otherwise.
- Help newcomers find their footing.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks.
- Discriminatory language or imagery.
- Publishing anyone's private information without their permission.
- Sharing confidential data from any organization.
- Any conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, commits, or other contributions that break this code, and may restrict or ban participants who repeat the behavior.

Report concerns privately to the maintainer through the contact listed on the maintainer's GitHub profile. Reports are handled confidentially.
